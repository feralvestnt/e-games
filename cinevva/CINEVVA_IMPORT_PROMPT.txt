PRIMARY GOAL:
A player familiar with the real present-day Cerro del Castell de Castalla should recognize the hill/castle relationship immediately.

Preserve:
- the compact rocky summit;
- steep western and southern escarpments;
- gentler east/southeast approach;
- current castle registered directly on the summit;
- the overall ridge silhouette;
- switchback-style access on the east/southeast slope.

Use realistic Mediterranean limestone, dry soil, sparse scrub and Aleppo pine vegetation.
Do not cover major rock faces with dense forest.
Do not flatten the summit beyond the supplied geometry.
Do not invent a generic fantasy mountain.

IMPORTANT ACCURACY RULE:
This package is a controlled present-day topographic REFERENCE reconstruction, not a raw LiDAR survey.
Do not claim centimetric accuracy and do not introduce fine terrain details that contradict the supplied shape.
