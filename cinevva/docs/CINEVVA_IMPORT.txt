CASTALLA CURRENT - CLEAN IMPORT

START FROM AN EMPTY PROJECT / EMPTY SCENE.
DO NOT USE ANY PREVIOUS CASTALLA FILES.

1) TERRAIN
Use:
terrain/heightmap_16bit.png
and:
terrain/terrain.json

Create terrain using EXACTLY:
width X = 900 meters
depth Z = 900 meters
vertical height range = 161.794 meters
Y-up
world origin = 0,0,0

IMPORTANT:
- do not flatten;
- do not normalize terrain height;
- do not rescale it to fit the scene;
- do not replace it with a generic hill;
- do not infer terrain from text.

2) CASTLE
Load:
castle/castle.glb

Read:
castle/placement.json

The castle geometry already contains its final spatial coordinates.
Use:
position 0,0,0
rotation 0,0,0
scale 1,1,1

IMPORTANT:
- DO NOT use drop-to-ground;
- DO NOT auto-center;
- DO NOT move the castle in Y;
- DO NOT fit castle to terrain;
- DO NOT recompute its transform.

3) VALIDATION
Before adding vegetation, materials, NPCs, roads, lighting effects or gameplay:
- confirm the hill has strong vertical relief;
- confirm the castle touches / penetrates the summit;
- confirm there is no visible gap under the castle;
- confirm the castle is not floating.

4) REFERENCE
terrain/terrain_reference.glb is visual reference only.
Use it only to compare shape if the generated heightmap terrain looks wrong.

STOP after validation.
Do not beautify or modify geometry until the terrain/castle relationship is confirmed.
